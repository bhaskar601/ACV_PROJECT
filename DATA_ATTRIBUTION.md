# Dataset attribution

The experiment uses Oxford-IIIT Pet images and human trimaps by O. M. Parkhi, A. Vedaldi, A. Zisserman and C. V. Jawahar, described in *Cats and Dogs*, CVPR 2012.

Official source: https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/pets/

Image mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/timm/oxford-iiit-pet/tree/089695c834a7deb60505b7cc506672db1c31a6aa

The official dataset page lists Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Copyright in source photographs remains with their original owners. RGB figures are resized derivatives, masks are recoloured derivatives, and prediction panels are outputs of this experiment. The dataset-derived example figures are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Exact source IDs are printed under the panels and listed in `artifacts/selected_examples.json` and `artifacts/data_manifest.json`.

This attribution applies to included dataset examples; the original full dataset is downloaded separately and excluded from the submission archive.
